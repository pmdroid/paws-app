Mode: Operate. Repository members inspect an exact candidate's checks and published media, including failures, without losing their chosen attempt, stream, expansion or playback during polling.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit DESIGN.md's lavender canvas, paper panels, orange actions, solid outlines, offset shadows and shared appearance controls.

Closeout's heading aligns with attempt controls and Expand all; a single result banner precedes one-line check summaries. Output streams switch within a dark terminal. The evidence gallery follows checks.

FINISH: Inspect passed/failed runs, gallery, lightbox, evidence and denied access at desktop and phone widths in both themes. Require repository checks and independent review, with screenshots retained for the pull request.
